Physician Biography & Academic Pedigree

Restoring Oral Health, Aesthetics & Confidence with Clinical Precision

Dr. Akshay Muchate is a dedicated dental surgeon specializing in Oral & Maxillofacial Prosthodontics, Crown and Bridge, and Oral Implantology. He completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (B.D.S.) from MIDSR Dental College, Latur under the prestigious Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik, and is actively pursuing his Master of Dental Surgery (M.D.S.) in Oral Maxillofacial Prosthodontics at the same institution.

He worked as an Intern (08/2023 – 09/2024) and is currently a Junior Resident (since 09/2025) at the Maharashtra Institute of Dental Science and Research (MIDSR), Latur — a NAAC Accredited 'A' Grade institution.

Dr. Muchate holds an active license with the Maharashtra State Dental Council (Reg. No: A-59790) and is a member of the Indian Prosthodontics Society. His skills include dentistry, health education, patient counseling and nutritional counseling, and he communicates in Marathi, Hindi, and English.
